About

Music and health in harmony. For everyone.

Memory Tracks Ltd is a UK social enterprise, founded in 2017, that uses personally meaningful music to support health and wellbeing.

Our story

The idea began with research at Liverpool Hope University, which linked memorable songs to daily routines for people living with dementia. Founder Gordon Anderson saw how the same idea could support care at scale.

We developed the Memory Tracks app through pilot studies with care staff and residents at Pendine Park and in RMBI care homes. We then took the method to NHS Highland, where MedTracks was tested for taking medication on time.
