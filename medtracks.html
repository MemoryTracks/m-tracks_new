Song-task association

Why music works

The songs we hear growing up stay with us for life. MedTracks uses that connection. When a familiar song is linked to a daily task, hearing it becomes a prompt to act.

We call this song-task association. It is the idea behind everything Memory Tracks builds, and it has been tested with NHS Highland.

What you get

  • Songs chosen for you

    Tell us your year of birth and MedTracks selects hit songs from when you were aged 6 to 15.

  • Or choose your own

    Pick from the Top 40 of every year from 1968 to 2019, or tap Surprise Me for a different song each time.

  • A song clip at reminder time

    Your reminder arrives with a 30-second clip of your song, even when the app is closed.

  • Taken or snooze

    Confirm a dose from the notification or in the app, or snooze the reminder for a few minutes.

  • Easy to recognise

    Add a photo of your medicine or its box, or pick a picture, so each reminder is easy to spot.

  • Your schedule

    Set the days, times and an optional end date for each medicine.

The evidence

Tested with NHS Highland

In a 30-day study with NHS Highland, 31 adults aged 40 and over reported forgetting their medication significantly less often. Music-based reminders were the most discussed part of the app, and 77% of interview comments about them were positive.
