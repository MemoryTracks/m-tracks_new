MedTracks

Your favourite songs, turned into medication reminders.

MedTracks links the music that engages you to the medicines you take, helping you stay on track every day. Built on peer-reviewed research with NHS Highland.

Work with us

MedTracks is available in the UK only.

How it works

  1. We find your songs.

    Tell us your year of birth and we select hit songs from when you were aged 6 to 15. Prefer to pick your own, or tap Surprise Me? You can do that too.

  2. Link each song to a medicine.

    Set the time and the song that goes with it.

  3. Hear the song, take the medicine.

    A familiar tune is harder to ignore than a standard alarm.

In the app

Swipe to see more →

  • Your song plays at reminder time. Mark the dose as taken, or snooze for 5, 10 or 15 minutes.

  • Swipe right to confirm. Each dose is marked as taken, so you can see your day at a glance.

  • Swipe left to change. Edit the time, dose or song, or remove a reminder.

Research partner

  • Peer-reviewed feasibility study with NHS Highland and three GP practices in Inverness
  • Significant improvement in forgetting to take medication after 30 days (31 participants, median age 69)
  • ORCHA score 87% independent assessment of health app quality
  • Funded by Innovate UK SBRI supporting the NHS Highland study

Research-led since 2017

Music, memory and everyday health

Memory Tracks Ltd has spent nine years studying how personally meaningful music can prompt everyday health tasks. We call the method song-task association.

We tested it first in UK care homes with people living with dementia, then with NHS Highland for medication adherence.
